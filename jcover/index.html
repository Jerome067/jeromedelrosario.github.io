Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. JCover works in guest mode with no sign-up.

Is my information stored?

No. JCover builds your cover page entirely in your browser, so your details never leave your device. Your draft is kept in your own browser so you do not lose work.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. The DOCX download is a normal Word document made of real text and tables.

Will it match my university's rules?

Templates are general-purpose. Please verify your institution's formatting requirements before submitting.

Does it support Bengali text?

Not in PDF yet. English text is fully supported.
