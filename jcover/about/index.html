About JCover

JCover helps university and college students create clean academic cover pages without fighting a word processor. It is designed first for students in Bangladesh and works for students anywhere.

Who it is for

Anyone submitting assignments, lab reports, project or internship reports, research papers, theses and case studies, individually or in groups.

Our approach to formatting

Every template has its own real PDF and Word renderer, built from the same layout definitions as the on-screen preview. The Word file is made of real text and tables so you can keep editing it. Templates are general-purpose, so please confirm your institution's own requirements before you submit.

Private by design

Everything happens in your browser. Your details and logo are never uploaded: the preview, the PDF and the Word file are all generated on your own device.

Credits
